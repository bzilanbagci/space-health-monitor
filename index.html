Sources and limits

  • Knowledge basis: the five hazards of human spaceflight from the NASA Human Research Program (HRP): space radiation, isolation and confinement, distance from Earth, gravity fields, and hostile closed environments. Descriptions are summarized in our own words.
  • Further reading: NASA HRP: Five Hazards and Human Research Roadmap.
  • Our own work: all sample values are synthetic, the indicator ranges are illustrative reference ranges chosen by our team, and the hazard tags are a simplified mapping. No NASA dataset is used for the numbers.
  • Image lab: the cell counter uses a simple threshold and connected-components method, for education only. Its sample image is either a NASA OSDR image (credited under the image) or a synthetic one, and it is labeled on screen.
  • Hazard Shields quiz: mini quiz and info card are general facts about the five HRP hazards, written in our own words.
  • Communication delay: the Mars delay of about 20 minutes each way is an approximation that varies with planet positions.
